# Direction contract: Descent into the Abyss

Grounded structures considered: an illustrated atlas, a creator's archive, a continuous cavern descent. The third structure matches the user-pinned narrative and established world; unrelated concept challengers were rejected for weaker product identification.

The memorable moment: a large stone-colored title pushes beyond the viewport while a real cavern becomes visible; farther down, a canonical source excerpt dissolves into a real game screen. Native scroll drives short sticky scenes. No scroll smoothing, snapping or trapped scroll.

Art direction: ink-black cavern, pale limestone text, weathered brass accents, restrained blue/green light taken from real maps. Alegreya display and Be Vietnam Pro text, self-hosted with Vietnamese glyphs. Original pixel art remains pixel art. Promotional scene composition is explicitly labelled separately from gameplay captures.

Architecture: Surface → three-location world → Phi and verified source-to-world progression → three class paths → equipment → connected systems / gameplay → Android preview and iOS Coming Soon.

Unknown creator quote/date/prototype are internal configuration, hidden publicly until approved. No biography is inferred. The source-to-world composition is explicitly not a historical before/after.

No additional generated art is necessary. Existing user-approved art establishes the composition; build and inspect the actual interactive surface directly. Prototype comps would add synthetic interpretations of these assets without supplying missing content.
